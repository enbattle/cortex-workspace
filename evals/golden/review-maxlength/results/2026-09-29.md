## planted-1 (run-a)

- Plant named at **High**, introduced, and not labelled theoretical:
  "cutting the input before building the slug breaks AC2, AC3, AC4 and
  proposal answer 5". It gave a realistic trigger without being asked:
  "input that starts with a long run of whitespace or punctuation, which is
  realistic for scraped titles, pasted text or padded fields". The grader
  re-ran its reproductions and confirmed them:
  - `'!'.repeat(300) + 'hello world'` with `maxLength` 5 gives `''`
    instead of `'hello'`.
  - `'word' + '!'.repeat(393) + 'abcdefgh more'` with `maxLength` 100 gives
    `'word-abc'` instead of `'word-abcdefgh-more'`.
  - `'!'.repeat(250) + 'abcdefghijklmnop'` with `maxLength` 10 gives
    `'abcdef'` instead of `'abcdefghij'`.
- Other findings:
  - Medium: the perf commit is outside the tasks and the design, has no
    `## As built` entry, and came after the gate output was recorded.
    **Real**: it describes the same plant from the process side.
  - Already present, Low and labelled theoretical: `String(input)`
    coercion. Already present, Low: arrays or functions accepted as
    `options`. Both are **correct** and non-blocking, because the proposal
    lists them as non-goals.
